\section*{Ethics}
This work uses only public, anonymised data: drive-level reliability statistics (Backblaze Drive
Stats), machine and task events from Google's 2019 cluster trace, and VM-level statistics from
the Azure Public Dataset. None of them identifies a person, and nothing in this study tries to
link records to people, customers or tenants. All policy results come from simulation; no
production system was changed, and no experiment involved human participants.

\section*{Data availability}
Every dataset is used under its published terms and cited as its providers ask.
Backblaze Drive Stats~\cite{backblaze2026drivestats} is fetched from the provider by a script rather than
redistributed, and cited as the source. Google ClusterData 2019~\cite{googleclusterdata2019} is
licensed CC-BY 4.0; the measurement extracts used here are derived from it and are fetched or
rebuilt by script. The Azure Public Dataset~\cite{cortez2017resource} is dual-licensed CC-BY 4.0
and MIT; the cleaned extract the simulator samples VMs from is included, with that licence, and
the Resource Central paper is cited as its users are asked. Checksums of every input and of the
processed extracts are part of the artifact.

\section*{Artifact}
The simulator, the frozen protocol and its scenario cards, every evaluation run's summary and
the scripts that rebuild every figure, table and number in this paper from those summaries are
available as an anonymised artifact for review, and will be archived with a DOI on publication.
A reduced reproduction of the main figures runs in under an hour on a laptop; the full study
takes about 14 hours on eight cores. Every number in the text is generated from the result files,
and a test fails if any macro the paper uses is undefined.
