## Week 15 (11-17 Jan): BAM-GB, its variants, and the Narya-inspired baseline

- [x] **P4, BAM-GB** (`vmsim/policies/bam.py`): b = p·c·e − m with survival-weighted c, queue-aware e recomputed after each pick (lazy heap; identical to a brute-force greedy on 40 random instances), w = GB, fill while b > 0; the engine reserves destination capacity as it goes
- [x] Variants: BAM-time (w = NIC-seconds), BAM-cost (w = m, ablation only)
- [x] Exact check (`scripts/v2/18_bam_exact_check.py`, scipy MILP/HiGHS; no new dependency): simulator snapshots of 200 VMs, gap mean 0.05%, p95 ≤ 0.30%, max 1.1%, 68% exactly optimal; synthetic, mean 0.19%, max 22% on one pathological instance (`results/v2/bam_exact_check.json`)
- [x] Ablation flags: `urgency=False` (e = 1), `c_uniform` (no criticality), `m_uniform` (uniform migration cost), `p_only`, `survival=False`
- [x] Identity test: p-only BAM with uniform c and m and no urgency equals P2 (tau = m / c) decision for decision, in three regimes and three within-host orders
- [x] **P5, Narya-inspired** (`vmsim/policies/narya.py`): simple (v5 spec: {nothing, migrate, avoid}, Beta posteriors, risk buckets) and faithful (NoOp / UA / UA-LM, Gamma-Poisson on cost, decay 0.99, 10% exploration, 14-day window); sticky per host; delayed, censored feedback from the engine's daily `Feedback`
- [x] `docs/v2/narya_differences.md`: every difference, row by row
- [x] Behaviour smoke (`scripts/v2/19_behaviour_smoke.py`, seeds 101-105, A3, 2%): BAM uses 4.6% of the budget (about 109 migrations a day; b > 0, not the budget, limits it); P5 2.1%; P5-faithful 0.7%, no migration on 46% of days, rejected arrivals doubled by avoid-allocation. Not the winner, by design
- [x] Engine: avoid-allocation marks, daily feedback, per-run `reset()` for stateful policies
- [~] **Finding for the theory (Week 17):** on single smoke worlds BAM migrates far more than the oracle and can cost more than never migrating. b = p·c·e − m treats today as the only chance to act with a 30-day p, but decisions recur daily and most VMs leave before their host fails, so waiting has value the formula ignores. It also ignores the destination's risk. BAM is implemented as specified; this is input to THEORY.md's assumptions, not a change to P4.

## Week 16 (18-24 Jan): uncertainty, drift, and P6

- [x] **Uncertainty on p** (`scripts/v2/21_uncertainty_drift.py --ensemble`, `results/v2/uncertainty_w16.json`): 20 boosted models on Poisson bootstraps of training drives, each calibrated on validation; 5-95% interval per test drive-day. Median relative interval width 0.48 (wider in the upper deciles, up to about 2×); the single Week 7 model falls outside the ensemble interval on 8.6% of rows. **The ensemble mean is much better than the single model: PR-AUC 0.131 against 0.074** (ROC-AUC 0.898 against 0.884; slope 1.16, slightly under-dispersed). Bagging stabilises the top of the ranking. This is on the test period, so it informs the model choice (decision for you) but is not a selection. The risk-averse BAM variant waits for the model-based predictor.
- [x] **Drift study** (`--drift`, `results/v2/drift_w16.json`): train on 2019..Y, Y = 2020-2024; test Y+1 and Y+2. The reliability slope falls with distance: 0.84-0.99 one year ahead, **0.76-0.97 two years ahead**; ECE up to 2.3 × 10⁻³ (base rate about 1.2 × 10⁻³). Rolling recalibration (isotonic on the last quarter before the test year) recovers two years ahead to slope 0.90-0.99 and ECE 1.7-6.3 × 10⁻⁴. PR-AUC 0.05-0.10, varying more by year than by distance
- [x] **Policy-cost effect of drift** (`--policy`, `results/v2/drift_policy_w16.json`): the median slopes mapped to the simulator's miscalibration (gamma = 1 / slope), BAM at A3, seeds 101-105: +0.97% cost without recalibration and +0.49% with it, against calibrated; per-seed signs are mixed, so the effect is small next to seed noise. A bridge until the simulator scores replayed drives with the model
- [x] **P6** (`vmsim/policies/guard.py`): plug-in Δ̂ of BAM over P2 per host with a host bootstrap; looks every 7 days with alpha_k = alpha 6/(pi² k²); calibration audit on matured outcomes in a rolling 90-day window (reliability slope in [0.8, 1.25], at least 30 events); BAM after two consecutive passing looks, back to P2 at the first failure; every look and switch logged
- [x] **Regime D** (rho_L 0.1 → 0.6 at mid-run with calibrated arrival blends; gamma 1 → 0.7 over the second half) and the **pure-noise control** (scores shuffled across hosts daily), unit-tested for their intended paths
- [~] **Smoke, superseded 4 Oct:** the numbers below used the cost-tuned P2 (`results/v2/superseded/guard_smoke_w16_p2c*`). Under ADR-005 P6's base is the budget-tuned P2; the rerun is in progress.
