## What is new, stated narrowly

1. **Not new: the decision rule.** Expected-cost thresholding is Elkan (2001); v1's rule is that threshold with example-dependent costs. Production work (Narya, NENYA, Deoxys) already shows cost-aware and causal mitigation exists at Microsoft, with no public data or code in the pages I read.
2. **New, if the plan delivers it:** (a) a measurement of where real placement sits relative to the break-even (the plan's measurement of the criticality-risk correlation), (b) the break-even as a surface over correlation, spread and estimation error with a closed form checked by simulation, (c) a budget-aware policy with a guard that stands down when evidence is weak, and (d) an open benchmark on public data.
3. **Risk to the novelty claim.** Deoxys was read on 28 Oct 2026 (`reading/zhang2024deoxys.md`): it is reactive, per node, with no budget, theory or public artifact, so (a), (b) and (d) stand. Its policy fallback is a precedent for (c): the guard is new only as a sequential, error-controlled switch with a calibration audit, and the paper must say so and cite it. NENYA is still abstract only (ACM, saved by hand).
