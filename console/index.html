S3 · Policy Lab

Why does a policy move this VM, or not?

One VM on one host, through each rule. BAM moves a VM when its expected benefit b = p·c·e − m is positive and its density b / w clears the day's cut-off. The threshold moves it when the host's p clears τ, whatever the VM is.

S5 · Paired Seeds

Does every seed agree?

The 30 pre-registered comparisons, one row each, with every evaluation seed as a dot. A comparison wins when its mean Δ against the tuned threshold P2 is at least 1% and its Holm-adjusted p is below 0.05.
